Contrapunctia is a micronation I founded on November 13th, 2025. It's official language is Interslavic (modified, see below) and it is located in my house and backyard. 

Contrapunctian Interslavic (also called "Contrapunctian" or "Contrapunctian Slavic") differs from Standard Interslavic in six major ways: Firstly, like in Polish, perfect tense verbs are marked for person AND gender, including for plural forms (virility). Secondly, conditional and pluperfect tenses (that use the by and bě partcles in SIS) append the particles to the verb (also like in Polish). Thirdly, the imperfective future tense is synthetic and is marked with a -č- in place of the infinitive -ti with -e-type personal endings appended (like in South Slavic languages). Fourthly, the present tense conjugation of perfective verbs is always the future perfective tense, like in North Slavic languages. Fifthly, there are future participles (perfective present and imperfective/iterative future via -či in place of infinitive). Finally, the vocative case, dual number, and the past simple tense are mandatory. As a side note, aspectual variations of a single verb are counted as conjugations of the verb to more accurately reflect and scale the complexity of Slavic verbal morphology to systems used by Western languages, which often encode aspect via tense.
But why? Why would I modify Interslavic, which aims to be a neutral ground between all Slavic languages, so much? The answer is disappointing if you were expecting something smart — it's really just to be more efficient, and also so it looks more complex on paper ;)
The three official scripts are Latin, Cyrillic, and Glagolitic.
